FAQ

What everyone asks us

Do I have to sort my clothes or be at home?

No. Put everything in a closed bag. We sort by colour and fabric. We just need someone to open the door in your slot, or a doorman or neighbour you trust.

Does vacuum-packing crease pressed clothes?

We use a partial vacuum and fold with paper separators. Shirts go on top. If you would rather we skip the packing, tell us when you order.

What don't you wash?

Dry-clean-only garments, leather, suede, suits and formal dresses. If something like that is in the bag, we return it separately, unwashed and free of charge.

What if a garment gets damaged?

We photograph every bag on arrival. We are insured and cover the value of the garment under our terms.

How do I pay?

By card or Bizum when we deliver. Subscriptions are billed at the start of the month.
