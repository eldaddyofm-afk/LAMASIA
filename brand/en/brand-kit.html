Costa Clean · Brand kit2
The brand

What Costa Clean is and how it speaks

The idea in one sentence

Your clothes come home the way you wish they always were: pressed, folded, vacuum-packed and scented the way you like. And they arrive by bike.

Promise

Leave a bag at the door and within 24-48 hours your laundry is back, ready for the wardrobe. No sorting, no waiting, no vans.

Who it is for

People aged 28 to 50 in the Centro, Soho, Malagueta and the east side of the city who value their time and well-cared-for clothes. Second audience: holiday rental managers.

What makes us different

Vacuum packing. Nobody in Málaga offers it. It is visual, it is understood in a second and it feels like new clothes.

The scent. Six options. It turns a chore into a choice.

The ironing. Mr Jeff and the rest only wash and fold.

The bike. Speed in the Centro and a friendly, local image.

Values

Care, closeness, cleanliness (visual too), Mediterranean pace.

Tone of voice

YesClose and direct, like a neighbour who knows their trade. Short sentences. First names. We talk about the clothes, not the laundry. "Your clothes come back pressed and smelling of sea breeze."
NoNo technical jargon ("textile sanitisation process"), no forced jokes, no strings of exclamation marks, no vague promises ("premium quality guaranteed"). Never "industrial laundry".

Brand lines

Main claim: Pressed, vacuum-packed and scented the way you like it. At your door.

Short: Your clothes the way you wish they always were.

For the bike and the bag: We take your laundry for a ride.

Costa Clean · Brand kit3
Logo

The hanger and the wave: one line, two ideas

Symbol on its own: WhatsApp and Instagram avatar (brand/en/avatar.svg), favicon, small stickers. Minimum size 8 mm / 24 px.

Rules

The symbol is a hanger whose bar is a wave: cared-for clothes and the coast in a single stroke. One line weight, never filled.
Clear space: the height of the hook on every side.
Symbol in Sea and type in Ink on light backgrounds; all white on Sea or on a photo with a 70 % Sea overlay.
Versions: horizontal (preferred), vertical (squares and social media), symbol only (avatar, favicon, small labels). Type converted to outlines: the printer does not need the font.

Incorrect uses

Do not rotate or tilt.
Do not stretch.
Do not change the colours or fill the hanger.
Do not place it on colours outside the palette.

This week

Seven things to get started

1. Get a WhatsApp Business line and replace +34 600 000 000 on the landing page, the cards and the kit.

2. Register the domain and point it to the landing page. Share the landing page and the beta from the Share menu so they open without an account.

3. Create the Instagram account with the symbol as the avatar and post three photos: a vacuum-packed bag, the bike and a pressed shirt.

4. Wash ten orders for friends and time everything: folding, ironing, packing. Those minutes are the business.

5. Request machinery quotes from three distributors (Girbau Málaga, Primer, Fagor) using the list on page 11, both new and on a rental plan.

6. Visit five premises in Perchel and Cruz de Humilladero and ask about the licence before signing.

7. Print 250 cards and 200 flyers with the QR code and hand them out in Soho and Malagueta in one afternoon.
